What happens after a press

Sending can briefly show a busy mark. A definitive refusal offers Retry and Cancel; an uncertain connection keeps the same attempt pending. Those states depend on real delivery, so this gallery does not paint pretend failures. Sent, Picked up, and Active describe the handoff to an agent, not a second action.
