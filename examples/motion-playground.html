What the recordings establish

These recordings capture the real Leaf panel from the original motion study. Each candidate changes its movement’s duration and easing. They were recorded slowly and retimed to nominal speed for comparison; they illustrate the proposed timing, rather than measure performance. The recorded panels cannot be operated.

The baseline is the version recorded for that study, not a promise about the panel in the Leaf version serving this page. Playback position and speed only change inspection; the playground sends the chosen timing.
