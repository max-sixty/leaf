Use `lf-visual-review` for a short website journey whose important states can be
compared as aligned stills, and describe in prose any motion that matters. Write the
claim and recommendation in the page's prose; the bound `visual-run` source supplies the
cases, their captured revisions and images, the observed results and the capture
conditions.

A page that is only the review is a workspace, `<main class="layout-workspace
density-working">`, where the review fills the window. In a document page, the review is
as tall as its evidence and the page scrolls past it. Don't put the review in a
scrolling box of your own.

Each case has a Looks right checkbox and its own On me obligation, which approval
completes and clearing or undoing approval reopens. Feedback belongs in threads, opened
from the case or from a selected passage or image area. A comment hands that case to the
agent without approving it, and approval doesn't resolve a thread.

Give a base or candidate URL only when its captured revision can actually be opened
there, and link a case's `traceUrl` when a trace helps diagnose it. An archive-only run
keeps its captures and shows no Open links.
