Use `lf-visual-review` for a short website journey whose important states can be compared as aligned stills. Put the claim and recommendation in authored prose. The bound `visual-run` supplies the ordered cases, immutable targets, image paths, observed results, and capture conditions.

For a focused review page, make `lf-visual-review` the body of a workspace page, `<main class="layout-workspace density-working">`, after an optional `header`. Where the workspace is full-height, the review fills it: the navigation and case details stay in view and the evidence stage takes the height left. A smaller window, or a review in a document with prose around it, keeps the same order in ordinary flow, and its evidence stage chains scrolling into the document at either end. Do not cap every visual object in another page-local scroller.

The widget compares stills. Describe motion that matters in prose; a case's `traceUrl` links a trace when one helps diagnose it.
