Use `lf-visual-review` for a short website journey whose important states can be compared as aligned stills. Put the claim and recommendation in authored prose. The bound `visual-run` supplies the ordered cases, immutable captured revisions, image paths, observed results, and capture conditions.

The review is as tall as its evidence and the page scrolls through it, so give a page that is only the review a wide page, `<main class="layout-wide density-working">`, rather than a workspace that would hold it in a smaller pane. Do not cap a visual object in a page-local scroller.

The widget compares stills. Describe motion that matters in prose; a case's `traceUrl` links a trace when one helps diagnose it.

Lead with the captured evidence and let the reader reach the verdict after inspecting both images. Keep capture conditions and comparison controls available without delaying that first reading.

Include a base or candidate URL only when its captured revision has a real replayable destination. Archive-only runs retain their revisions and captures without Open controls.
