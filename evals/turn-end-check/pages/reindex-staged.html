Rebuilding the search index

Staging serves the rebuilt index, search-v7.

Plan

  1. Rebuild all five shards on staging and swap the alias.
  2. Compare p95 latency and result counts with the old index.
  3. Rebuild production, one region at a time.

Fallback plan

If production error rates rise after a region's swap, roll it back to the old cluster, es-legacy:

  1. Point that region's search alias back at es-legacy.
  2. Replay the writes logged since the swap into es-legacy.
  3. Keep es-legacy up for two weeks after the last region swaps.
