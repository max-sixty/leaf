Search index rebuild

The product search index is rebuilt onto the new mapping, then traffic moves to it.

Plan

The rebuild copies documents in batches of 500 and pauses two seconds between batches.

Cutover to the new index is scheduled for 21 October.

Rehearsal

The rerun on 18 September, after the mapping change, rebuilt 4.3 million documents in 3 h 05 min.

How should traffic move to the new index?

Every approach keeps the old index until the new one has served a full day.

Alias swap Point the search alias at the new index in one step. My take: the fastest, and the rehearsal shows the new index keeps up. Shadow reads first Send a copy of every query to the new index for a day, then swap. Tenant by tenant Move tenants to the new index in groups of ten.
