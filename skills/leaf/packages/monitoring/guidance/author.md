Use `lf-monitor` when the reader must keep one stable overview, a changing evidence stream, and one exception decision in view together. Its sole body is an `lf-monitor-grid` containing one `lf-monitor-region` for each `zone`: `overview`, `evidence`, then `exception`. Give every region a stable id and label. Put current external evidence in a data-backed widget such as `lf-source`; keep the exception's premise and `lf-ask` in authored markup so a feed update cannot change the decision being answered.
