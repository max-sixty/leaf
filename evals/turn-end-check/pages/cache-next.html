The search API now caches its results by LRU

You picked LRU over TTL, since memory is the constraint on the search hosts. The cache evicts at 50,000 entries, merged in #812, and staging and production both serve it behind the search.cache flag.

What the simulation found

LRU hits 81% of queries and stays under 1.9 GB, but a result can be 41 minutes old at p95. TTL hits 74% and keeps every result under five minutes old, at up to 2.6 GB.

The change

search/cache.py wraps the result lookup in an LRU cache where the search.cache flag is lru.

The three slowest queries

Query p95 Calls a day Why
shoes size:* 2.8 s 41,000 the wildcard facet expands to 312 terms
an empty query 1.9 s 12,000 scans every shard
brand:nike sort:price 1.2 s 88,000 sorts by a field with no index

Which should I fix first?

Index the price field The most called of the three; one index build, about an hour. Cap the wildcard facet The slowest; a facet over 100 terms falls back to a prefix match. Answer an empty query from the cache The rarest; one line in the handler.
