Orders database cutover

The orders database moves from the shared cluster to its own cluster this quarter. This page holds the plan, the rehearsal results, and the choice of cutover mode.

Schedule

The migration window opens on 14 October at 02:00 UTC and lasts four hours.

Rollback

Rollback plan

If replication lag exceeds five minutes, Priya Raman owns the rollback and restores traffic to the shared cluster.

Attempts

The current attempt copies shards in parallel and verifies each checksum before switching reads.

The earlier attempt copied shards one at a time and stopped at shard 37 when the disk filled.

Which cutover mode should we use?

All three modes finish inside the window; they differ in what a failure costs.

Big bang Stop writes, copy everything, switch once. My take: the simplest to run. Shard by shard Move and switch one shard at a time. Dual write Write to both clusters, then switch reads.
