Use `lf-visual-review` for a short website journey whose important states can be compared as aligned stills. Write the claim and recommendation in the page's prose. The bound `visual-run` source supplies the cases, their captured revisions and images, the observed results and the capture conditions.

A page that is only the review is a workspace, `<main class="layout-workspace density-working">`, where the review fills the window. In a document page, the review is as tall as its evidence and the page scrolls past it. Don't put the review in a scrolling box of your own.

The widget compares stills, so describe motion that matters in prose. A case's `traceUrl` links to a trace when one helps diagnose it.

Lead with the captured evidence, and let the reader reach the verdict after looking at both images.

Give a base or candidate URL only when its captured revision can actually be opened there. An archive-only run keeps its captures and shows no Open links.
