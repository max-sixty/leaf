A comment now survives opening its screenshot

Pressing Escape on a screenshot's preview used to close the page comment it was attached to. I recorded one journey before and after the fix. Choose a moment, then switch recordings to see the same step in the other.

Ship the fix?

The recordings part at Back from the preview. Before the fix the comment is gone and focus is on the page; after it the comment is back with its words and screenshot, and the thumbnail holds focus.

The recordings show Escape in Chrome. The regression test also closes the preview with its close button and with a press outside it, in the page comment and in a thread reply in the floating panel, and fails on each without the fix.

Why the comment closed

The page comment card is an auto popover, and opening a modal dialog closes every auto popover the dialog does not stand in. The image preview is a modal dialog, so opening it closed the card, and closing it left focus on the page. The preview now stands inside the open card while it is shown and returns to its usual place when it closes.
