Rebuilding the search index

Staging serves the rebuilt index, search-v7; production is next.

Plan

  1. Rebuild all five shards on staging and swap the alias.
  2. Compare p95 latency and result counts with the old index.
  3. Rebuild production, one region at a time.

Fallback plan

If production error rates rise after the swap, point the alias back at the old cluster, es-legacy, which stays up for two weeks for that purpose.
