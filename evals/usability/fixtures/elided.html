Order-history backfill schedule

The order-history backfill copies 310 million rows into the warehouse over eleven nights.

Schedule

The copy runs each night in the maintenance window, at full speed, in batches of 500 rows.

The first night is 6 October; the last is 16 October.

Safety

The replica absorbs the write load; the soak test showed it keeps up with room to spare.

If a night's batch fails, the job stops and the next night resumes from the last committed batch.
