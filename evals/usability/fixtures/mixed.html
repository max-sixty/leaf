Order-history backfill

Fourteen months of order history move into the warehouse before the reporting cutover. Pick how the copy runs.

Plan

The backfill copies 310 million rows of order history from the primary database into the warehouse.

A dry run over one month of orders finished without errors.

The reporting team moves its dashboards to the warehouse on 3 November, and every dashboard that reads order history needs the full fourteen months to be there first, because the year-over-year comparisons that finance reviews each month reach back that far, and a partial history would show those comparisons as sharp drops that nobody could tell apart from real ones.

How should the copy run?

Online, throttled Copy in batches during business hours at 20% of the replica's capacity. No maintenance window; about nine days. Nightly window Copy at full speed in the 01:00–03:00 UTC maintenance window. Eleven nights. Snapshot restore Restore last night's snapshot into the warehouse in one step. Needs a 40-minute write freeze on the primary.
