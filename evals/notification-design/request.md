record my final choice with the actual selected design still visible.

- Headline: Payments are processing slowly

- Format: Callout or Status strip. Start with Callout. Make the formats visibly

settings summary, so I can compare the appearance while tuning it. Keep the controls
and summary beside the preview in a 900px-wide window, including a short window,
and make the page usable on a phone. Choose the typography, colors and other
styling yourself; preserve readable content in both formats and densities.

Give me a clear way to finish choosing. After I finish, the page should record the
final settings and show the notification in that appearance, rather than leaving
only a verbal description of it. Recording the design does not mean it has shipped.
