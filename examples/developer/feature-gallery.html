On me: decisions and answers

An lf-ask puts a heading and context around the choice below. On me lists everything waiting on you: open obligations, a question in a thread among them (such as the gallery thread about a pasted image), to-dos the agent put on you, and updates whose reply failed and need sending again.

  • Press m: the page moves to the next item on you in page order, and Shift+M walks back. Where that item is in another view, it appears at once and the view's tab name briefly highlights; with reduced motion, there is no glide or highlight.
  • Look at Leaf's panel at the window's foot: the On me mark beside Threads counts what is on you.
  • Press it, or g then Shift+M: the On view opens above the panel's row on On me, with a tab for the agent's list, such as On Claude, each tab counting its list. What left each list stands under it as Done: answered obligations under On me, sent replies and ended to-dos under the agent's. Press Enter on a row: it goes where m would.
  • Find the sample picnic booking among the obligations. Its row takes you to the request, where a pick answers it and pressing it again reopens it.
  • Comment on the map question, then press m until it lands there: the discussion stays closed while you read and choose, even when Tab enters the controls. Press c to reply in that discussion, t to read it, or, under a finger, its Thread marker to open it. Answer the question and come back to it: its controls stay clear.

              Drafts render Markdown as readable prose. Clicking the words, or the
              pencil on the box’s edge, which shows while the pointer is over the box,

              Escape discards the unsaved edit. The box keeps its size while
              editing, and these controls stay when annotations are hidden. Copy, beside
              them, copies its Markdown. Press z to undo a saved edit.
