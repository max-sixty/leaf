private security report · Nadia, Nov 9

Open an advisory for redirects into internal networks?

marram follows redirects wherever they lead, so a docs link that redirects to 169.254.169.254 makes CI request the cloud metadata service and log its status. The body never reaches the log: an attacker learns which internal hosts answer, not what they hold.

Reproduced
1.7.2 on a self-hosted EC2 runner logged 200 OK
Nadia asks
a CVE, and public disclosure on Dec 9
Fix
refuse redirects from a public address into a private or link-local one unless --allow-private is set

I'd open the advisory: the fix is small and fits 1.7.3. A team whose public links redirect into its own network, such as a sign-on page, would need the flag.
