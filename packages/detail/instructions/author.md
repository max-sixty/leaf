Use `lf-dial` on a page long enough that a reader might want only its conclusions, such
as a report, an investigation or a plan. At Skim the reader sees the title, the lede, the
headings and the blocks marked `.gist`, and nothing else, so write the page so that those
alone carry its argument.

Open each section with its gist: a sentence or two saying what the section concludes,
not what it covers, written so it stands on its own. "The cache key changed, so no
cached cart matched" is a gist; "This section looks at the cache" is not. The rest of the
section supports the gist without restating it, since at Read the reader sees both. Mark
a question the reader must not miss at Skim `.gist` too, such as an `lf-ask` they owe an
answer.

Put material only some readers want, such as method, raw output or an alternative you
ruled out, in a `details` with a summary naming it. Read leaves it closed and Everything
opens it.
