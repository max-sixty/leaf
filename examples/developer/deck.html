a single choice

Which session store ships in 2.4?

Postgres removed the Redis hop in load tests, but the move needs a migration two days before the release. I'd keep Redis for 2.4 and move in 2.5: 12 ms at p99 isn't worth a migration this close to Friday.

a reply to send

Send this reply to the support thread about slow sign-ins?

2.4 caches avatar lookups on the sign-in page, which cut sign-in from 2.1 s to 0.6 s in load tests. The reply goes out under your name, with any edit you make to it. I'd send it: the fix is merged, and the customer has waited a week. 

Thanks for your patience. 2.4 caches the lookups that slowed sign-in down; in our tests it went from about two seconds to well under one. It ships on Friday.

What to try

  • By keyboard, while a card waits: m lands on the open card's question, a digit answers it, and ← and → are Back and Later.
  • Answer the session store: the card slides off to the right and the upgrade note comes up. The marks above the cards turn green as each is answered.
  • Accept or reject the upgrade note from its entry in the margin; either moves the deck on.
  • Pick platforms for the beta: a multiple choice stays open until Done.
  • The code freeze asks nothing, so the pass goes past it; Back reaches it.
  • Press Later on the support reply, or swipe it left on a phone: it waits for the end of the pass, and Go through brings it back.
  • At the end of the pass, the frame lists each question beside its answer, in the words On Me uses for it, and each question links to its card.
