A result cache for the search API

Two eviction policies, simulated on last week's query log: LRU at 50,000 entries and TTL at five minutes.

What the simulation found

LRU hits 81% of queries and stays under 1.9 GB, but a result can be 41 minutes old at p95. TTL hits 74% and keeps every result under five minutes old, at up to 2.6 GB.

Method

Both policies replayed the 1.2 million queries logged from 26 October to 1 November through bench/cache_sim.py.
